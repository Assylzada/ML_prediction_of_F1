TASK 03

Data Preparation

Cleaning, missing values, outliers, encoding, scaling and splitting.

01

Missing Values

Continuous features: median imputation. Binary: filled with 0. Missing qualifying is flagged by quali_missing. Pit-lane starts (grid = 0) become the last grid slot.

02

Outliers

Continuous features are winsorized at the 1st and 99th percentile. Quantiles are learned on the training set only.

03

Encoding

grid_zone (pole, front row, top 6, top 10, midfield, back, pit lane) is One-Hot encoded. Driver and team names are not encoded: they are replaced by form features.

04

Scaling

Continuous features are standardized with StandardScaler (important for Logistic Regression and KNN).

CHRONOLOGICAL TRAIN / TEST SPLIT
TRAIN · 80%
TEST · 20%

Train = earlier races, test = the most recent races. The model is always evaluated on the "future", like in real life.

STATISTICAL SIGNIFICANCE

Is the difference between models real?

The test set contains a limited number of races, so a gap of one or two points can be pure luck. To check this, races were resampled with replacement 300 times (bootstrap) and 95% confidence intervals were computed. A model is only credited with beating the baseline if the whole interval of the difference lies above zero.

EVALUATION PROTOCOL

Why the comparison is fair

Same data: every model sees the same features, the same chronological split (earlier seasons for training, latest seasons for testing) and the same folds.

No leakage: all features are built only from information available before the race, and folds are grouped by race so that drivers of one race never appear in both training and validation.

Own preprocessing: each model has its own scaler and encoder fitted inside the pipeline on training data only.

Threshold: the decision threshold is selected on out-of-fold predictions, never on the test set.

METRICS EXPLAINED

How to read the numbers

PR-AUC (main selection metric): area under the precision-recall curve. It is robust to class imbalance (only 3 of about 20 drivers reach the podium) and does not depend on a threshold.

ROC-AUC: probability that a random podium finisher is ranked above a random non-podium driver.

F1: harmonic mean of precision and recall at the chosen threshold.

Top-3 hit rate: a race-level metric that matches the real task, picking the three drivers most likely to stand on the podium.
